In Step Challenge, you only see your friends' steps, and they only see
yours. To become friends, one sends an **invitation link**, the other
accepts it. There is no user search.

## Invite

1. **Leaderboard** tab, **Friends**, then **Invite friends**.
2. Share the link by message, WhatsApp, email… with one person or a
   whole group: everyone who accepts it becomes your friend.

The link is valid for **7 days**. In **Friends**, you see your active
links, how many people accepted them, and you can turn them off.

## Being invited

**You already have Step Challenge**: open the link, the invitation shows
up in the app. Accept it.

**You don't have Step Challenge yet**: open the link, then tap **Accept
the invitation**. Google Play opens:

1. install Step Challenge;
2. open it and sign in with your Google account;
3. the invitation shows up: accept it.

You don't need to go back to the message.

## The invitation doesn't show up?

The invitation page also shows a **code** (for example `K7F3-M9QX`). In
Step Challenge, **Leaderboard** tab, **Friends**, **I have a code**, then
enter it. This helps if you installed the app another way than with the
page's button (from AppGallery, for example).

- **"Invalid invitation link"**: the link expired (7 days) or was turned
  off. Ask for a new link.
- **The link opens in the browser although the app is installed**: tap
  **Accept the invitation**, or update the app from Google Play.
