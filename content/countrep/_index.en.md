CountRep is a free application to log, throughout the day, your
repetitions of bodyweight and resistance band exercises: push-ups,
pull-ups, squats, plank… A few reps here and there add up, and CountRep
keeps the count.

## Log in two taps

- Pick an exercise, enter your reps (or seconds, for the plank): done.
- Choose up to ten exercises for your day, each with its illustration.
- Prepare your **workouts**: a list of exercises with their target reps,
  repeated over several rounds. Start one and tick the rounds as you go.

## Follow your progress

- A **history** of each day, with your sets and workouts.
- **Statistics** over a day, a week, a month or a year, by exercise,
  with your streaks and a consistency chart over 16 weeks.

## Works everywhere, even offline

- **No account needed**: everything works on your phone, without a
  network connection.
- **Sign in with Google** if you want: your data is backed up and synced
  between your devices.
- Android app, and a web app that you can install on an iPhone or a
  computer.
- In English and French, light or dark theme.

## Respectful of your data

- Free, with no advertising and no tracking.
- Without an account, your data never leaves your phone.
- With an account, only a technical identifier of your Google account and
  your first name are kept, never your email address or photo.
- Delete your account and all your data at any time from the app's
  settings. See the [privacy policy]({{< relref "/countrep/privacy" >}}).
