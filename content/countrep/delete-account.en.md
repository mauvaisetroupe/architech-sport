This page explains how to delete your account in **CountRep**, the
repetition logging application published by Lionel Coquin, and what
happens to your data.

You only have an account if you signed in with Google in CountRep.
Without an account, your data is only on your device: uninstalling the
application deletes it.

## How to delete your account

**From the application** (immediate):

1. Open CountRep, on Android or at
   [countrep.architech.lu](https://countrep.architech.lu).
2. Go to the **Settings** tab (*Réglages*), then **Account and
   security** (*Compte et sécurité*).
3. Tap **Delete my account** (*Supprimer mon compte*).
4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

If you no longer have the Android application, use the web application:
sign in with the same Google account, then follow the same steps.

**By email**:

1. Send an email to
   [support@architech.lu](mailto:support@architech.lu?subject=CountRep%20account%20deletion)
   with the subject *"CountRep account deletion"*.
2. Send it from the email address of the **Google account you use to
   sign in** to CountRep, and include the first name shown in the app's
   settings, so that your account can be identified.
3. Your account is deleted within 30 days, and you receive a
   confirmation by email.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes, from the CountRep server:

- your account and your first name;
- the link between your account and your Google account;
- all your sets, workouts and exercise selection.

From the application, the data stored on the device you use is deleted
at the same time, and the application starts again as on first use.

## What is kept

- **Nothing linked to your account is kept on the CountRep server.**
  Deletion is immediate and permanent, and cannot be undone.
- Other devices still signed in to your account are signed out, but the
  data stored on them stays there until you delete it or uninstall the
  application.
- Your Google account is not affected. You can also remove CountRep from
  the apps connected to your Google account in your Google account
  settings.

You can create a new account later by signing in again; it starts empty.
