CountRep is an application to log daily repetitions of bodyweight and
resistance band exercises. It is available as an Android application and
as a web application at
[countrep.architech.lu](https://countrep.architech.lu).

## 1. Using CountRep without an account

CountRep works fully without an account. In that case, everything you
enter (sets, workouts, exercise selection, settings) is stored **only on
your device**, in the application's storage (or your browser's storage,
for the web application). Nothing is sent to the CountRep server, and
the application makes no network request to it.

This data stays on the device until you delete it, uninstall the
application or clear the browser's data for the web application.

## 2. Data collected when you sign in

Signing in is optional. It is used to back up your data and synchronize
it between your devices.

### Account information

Users sign in with their Google account. When signing in, Google provides
the application with a signed identity token. From this token, CountRep
stores only:

- The stable, technical Google account identifier (the OpenID Connect
  "subject"), used to recognize the user at their next sign-in;
- The first name of the Google account, shown to the user in the
  application's settings. It is not shown to anyone else.

CountRep does **not** store the user's email address, profile photo or
any other Google account information, and does not access any Google
service on the user's behalf.

After sign-in, the application keeps a session token on the device,
valid for 7 days and renewed while the application is used. The server
does not keep a list of sessions.

### Training data

Once signed in, the application synchronizes with the CountRep server:

- **Sets**: the exercise, the number of repetitions (or seconds), the
  date and time, and, for sets logged during a workout, the workout's
  identifier and name;
- **Workouts**: their name, number of rounds, exercises and target
  repetitions;
- **Exercise selection**: the exercises chosen for the logging screen,
  and their order.

CountRep does not access any other health or fitness data, and does not
read data from other applications.

### Technical data

Like any web server, the CountRep server records technical logs of the
requests it receives (date, address requested, IP address, response
code). They are used only to operate and secure the service, and are
kept for a limited time.

## 3. How data is used

Data is used only to provide CountRep's features: logging, history,
statistics, workouts, backup and synchronization between the user's
devices.

It is not used for advertising, profiling, marketing or medical
purposes. CountRep contains no advertising, no analytics and no
third-party tracking.

## 4. Data sharing

CountRep does not sell personal data and does not share it with
advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

No other user can see your data: CountRep has no social features.

Data may be processed by technical service providers required to operate
the service (hosting and network protection), only for that purpose.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

Synchronized data is kept as long as the account exists.

Users can delete their CountRep account at any time from the
application: **Settings → Account and security → Delete my account**.
This immediately and permanently deletes, on the server, the account,
its link to the Google account, its first name and all its training data
(sets, workouts, exercise selection). The data stored on the device is
deleted at the same time.

Other devices still signed in to the deleted account are signed out;
the data stored on them stays on those devices.

Users who no longer have the application can delete their account from
the web application, or request the deletion by email. Both procedures
are described on the
[account deletion page]({{< relref "/countrep/delete-account" >}}).

## 6. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Reasonable
technical and organizational measures are used to protect CountRep data
against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, users may have rights
including:

- Access to their personal data;
- Correction of inaccurate data;
- Deletion of their personal data;
- Withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent;
- Restriction or objection to certain processing;
- Data portability where applicable.

To exercise these rights, contact us (see the Contact section).

## 8. Children's privacy

CountRep is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from
children in violation of applicable law.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when CountRep introduces new
functionality or when legal or technical requirements change.

The latest version will always be published at the privacy policy URL
provided to users and application platforms.
